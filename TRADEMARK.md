# Trademark policy

"Halftone", the Halftone logo, and "gitbadger" are trademarks of
Artur Daschevici. The MIT and Apache-2.0 licenses cover the *code*;
they do not cover the *name*. Apache-2.0 §6 says this explicitly, and
the same rule applies to the MIT-licensed copy.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to refer to this project truthfully ("built with
  Halftone", "compatible with Halftone verdict schema v1").
- Redistribute **unmodified** official releases, binaries, and crates
  under their original name.
- Use `halftone-*` as a crate-name prefix only for crates published from
  the `gitbadger` GitHub organisation.

## You may not

- Ship a modified fork, derivative, or competing product under the name
  "Halftone" or a confusingly similar name (Halftone Pro, HalfTone,
  halftone-ng, …). Rename it.
- Publish crates named `halftone-*` on crates.io or elsewhere.
- Use the name or logo in a way that implies endorsement, affiliation,
  certification, or that your verdicts are "Halftone verdicts".
- Use the name for hosted services, training courses, or merchandise.

## Forks

Forking is expected and welcome under the code licenses. A fork must
pick its own name and remove the logo before it is distributed. It may
state "forked from Halftone" in its README.
